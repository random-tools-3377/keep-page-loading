The first version.

**What it does:** type a web address and the page opens in the app. It keeps running while the
screen is off, and after you leave the app, until you tap **Stop** in its notification.

- Sites that pause themselves when you look away, or when the screen goes off, are told they're
  still on screen and in use, so they carry on.
- If the connection drops, the page tries again on its own: after 30 seconds, then less often.
- If the phone closes the page to free memory, it loads it again.
- It remembers the last page and opens it next time.
- Back goes back a page. From the first page, Back leaves the app with the page still running.

**The first time you open a page** it asks two things. Say yes to both:

1. "Allow notifications": the notification is what lets the page keep running, and has the
   Stop button.
2. "Let app always run in background?": so the phone's battery saving doesn't put it to sleep.

**On some phones (Samsung, Xiaomi and others)** the phone's own battery saving can still close
it. If it stops overnight, tap ⋮ → **Battery settings**, then under Battery choose
**Unrestricted**.

**How to install:** download `keep-page-loading-0.1.0.apk` below on the phone, open it, and allow
installing from your browser if the phone asks.
