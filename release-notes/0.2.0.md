**Tabs.** Tap the square with a number in it, next to the address, to see your tabs, switch
between them, close them, or open a **New tab**. Every tab keeps running, with the screen off and
after you leave the app, not only the one you're looking at.

**Open links in a new tab.** Press and hold a link for **Open in new tab**, **Open in new tab,
stay here** or **Copy link address**. Links a site opens in a new window open in a new tab.

**Told when a page stops.** If nothing on a page changes for 5 minutes, and it isn't talking to
its server, while your connection is working, the phone buzzes and sounds once, and the
notification says "A page seems to have stopped". Tap it to go to that tab. When the page starts
changing again, the alert goes away.

The notification now shows each page and how it's doing: running, waiting for the connection,
or stopped.

To update, download `keep-page-loading-0.2.0.apk` below on the phone and open it: it installs
over the old one and keeps your page (it becomes your first tab) and your logins.
