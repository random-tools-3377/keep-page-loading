**Swiping the app away** from the recent-apps list now keeps the page running at full speed, the
same as turning the screen off or pressing Home. Open the app again and the same page is there,
still going.

While the app is swiped away, the hidden page is no longer drawn, which saves battery.

Everything else is the same as 0.1.0. To update, download `keep-page-loading-0.1.1.apk` below on
the phone and open it: it installs over the old one and keeps your page and logins.
